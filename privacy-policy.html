Privacy Policy for BMI Calculator

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BMI Calculator ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the BMI Calculator mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

BMI Calculator is designed to calculate Body Mass Index (BMI) based on information entered by the user, such as height, weight, age, and gender.

The BMI information entered into the calculator is used to provide the calculation and results within the app.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

2. Google Sign-In

The app may allow you to sign in using Google Sign-In. When you use Google Sign-In, Google may provide information such as your name, email address, and profile information according to the permissions granted during sign-in.

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate your account and provide account-related functionality within the app.

We do not sell or use your Google account information for advertising purposes.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide BMI calculations and related results.
  • Provide account authentication through Google Sign-In.
  • Maintain and improve the functionality of the application.
  • Respond to user requests or support inquiries.

4. Data Storage

BMI Calculator does not require you to provide personal information simply to calculate your BMI.

If you use Google Sign-In, authentication-related information may be processed by Google and by services used by the application to provide authentication functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services, including Google services, to provide authentication and other functionality.

These third-party services may collect and process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

BMI Calculator is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.
